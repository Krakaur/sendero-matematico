# Recursos visuales de la copa del río · 0.7.0

Creación: 13 de octubre de 2026, hora local de Chihuahua (14 de octubre UTC). Ilustraciones originales generadas con la herramienta integrada `image_gen` en modo `built-in`. No se reutilizan imágenes, personajes ni código de Arcademics. La generación no constituye una verificación jurídica de exclusividad.

Los PNG originales se incorporan sin retocar sus píxeles. La aplicación selecciona mediante CSS los cuatro cuadrantes del atlas y anima remos vectoriales originales, embarcaciones, estelas y cámara mediante código. Se sustituyó el recorte SVG por composición CSS para reducir el trabajo de dibujo. No hay análisis ni modificación del sistema sonoro en esta intervención.

## Prompt del paisaje

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: original background artwork for the playable river race in the children's math game Sendero Matemático. Create a richly illustrated professional 2.5D storybook game environment, wide landscape 16:9 composition. A sunlit turquoise forest river runs horizontally from left to right. Camera is a gently elevated lateral three-quarter view looking across the river, appropriate for small animal canoes racing to the right. The middle 60 percent of image HEIGHT must be generous calm uninterrupted turquoise and jade water, low contrast ripples, free of characters, boats, islands, text or obstacles; four racers and UI are overlaid by code. The TOP 25 percent contains a layered lush woodland shoreline: rounded leafy trees with dimensional foliage, roots, ferns, little golden flowers, soft pale distant mountains, warm shafts of afternoon light; a tiny waterfall in the far upper-right shoreline. The BOTTOM 12 percent has close foreground grasses, flowers, smooth stones and reeds framing the water. Sophisticated color hierarchy: deep petrol-green shadows, luminous turquoise water, moss and emerald foliage, warm honey-gold sunlight, small coral floral accents. Hand-painted stylized game-art finish, restrained fine brush texture, crisp designed silhouettes with rounded volumes, layered ambient occlusion, rich materials and soft highlights. Charming and polished, not preschool clipart, not flat SVG, not a photograph, not a UI mockup. Clear foreground/middle/background depth. No lettering, no numerals, no logos, no border, no racers, no boats, no interface. Background only, full bleed.
